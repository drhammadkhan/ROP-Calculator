How is the window calculated?

The calculator works out how many days after birth a baby reaches 31 weeks or 36 weeks corrected gestational age (CGA), compares that with 28 days after birth, then selects the later or earlier point according to the rules below. Birth is day 0. The window runs for 7 days (start day to start day + 6).

BabyWindow starts
Born at under 32 weeksWhichever is later: 31+0 weeks CGA or 28 days after birth
Born at 32 weeks or more, birthweight ≤ 1500 gWhichever is earlier: 36+0 weeks CGA or 28 days after birth (never before birth)
Born at 32 weeks or more, birthweight > 1500 gNot eligible

Screening eligibility: infants born under 31 weeks (up to 30+6) or weighing 1500 g or less (below 1501 g) should be examined. Infants born at 31+0 to 31+6 weeks with a birthweight above 1500 g should be considered for examination.

About this tool

This is a web version of the RCPCH ROP Screening Window Calculator (version 3.2, October 2024), which implements the revised 2024 UK Screening of Retinopathy of Prematurity (ROP) clinical guideline. The calculation is identical to the spreadsheet.

Guideline: rcpch.ac.uk – Screening of retinopathy of prematurity clinical guideline.

Nothing you enter leaves your device. Feedback on the original calculator: guidelines@rcpch.ac.uk.

Disclaimer. Results are indicative only and must never be used in place of, or in opposition to, guidance from qualified medical professionals. This web version is not an official RCPCH product; always refer to the current guideline.
